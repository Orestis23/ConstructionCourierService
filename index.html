What does Construction Courier Service deliver?

We provide construction material delivery for lumber, pipe, conduit, construction supplies, contractor materials and other jobsite items that fit safely within our pickup truck and rack capacity. Loads may weigh up to 1,000 pounds.

Call or submit the delivery request form for availability.

Do you pick up materials from suppliers?

Yes. Place the order with your preferred supplier, then provide the pickup location, jobsite and timing. We will confirm the route and deliver the order directly to your crew.

Your crew works. We deliver.

What delivery options are available?

Available options include same-day construction material delivery, rush jobsite delivery, after-hours delivery, next-business-day delivery and multi-stop delivery, subject to availability.

What areas do you serve?

We serve locations within 30 miles of Mishawaka, Indiana, including South Bend, Granger, Elkhart, Goshen, Niles and nearby communities. Custom quotes are available for longer routes.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on delivery type, distance, timing, route details, material size and whether the job requires rush, after-hours or multi-stop service. Pricing is confirmed before scheduling.
